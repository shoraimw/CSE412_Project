Presentation honesty / integration boundaries
bKash and Nagad transactions use manual transaction-ID submission and administrator verification; no automatic payment gateway is connected. SMS/WhatsApp links open the user's messaging app and require the user to press Send. SOS is recorded and shown to DRIVE administrators; it does not dispatch police or emergency services. GPS tracking requires the relevant browser workspace to remain open. Tourist and routing estimates depend on external map services and do not include live traffic. Do not use real payment details or treat the academic demo as an emergency service.
